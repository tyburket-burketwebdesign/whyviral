The method

Most tools tell you viral videos use hooks.

So do the ten thousand videos that got 400 views. A pattern only means something if ordinary videos don't share it — so we go and measure them.

What other tools do

Read three viral videos, list what they have in common, and call it insight. Everything they find is also true of videos that flopped.

What WhyViral does

Pull up to 50 ordinary videos from the same hashtag, keep the low performers, and count how often each trait appears there. "4.2× more common" is a measurement, not an opinion.

What's inside

Built to answer why, not just what.

Measured baseline

Real comparison group

Every analysis pulls ordinary videos from the same hashtag and counts trait rates there. Findings are labelled measured or estimated, so you always know which number to trust.

Spoken hook

Reads the audio, not just the caption

The hook is what viewers hear in the first two seconds, and it's frequently unrelated to the caption. WhyViral transcribes it and classifies it across eleven patterns.

Comment mining

What the audience actually said

Buying intent, price objections, doubt, compatibility questions. The objection that repeats across the set gets written into every script as its own beat.

Engagement math

Share and save rate, not likes

Shares push a video into new networks and saves signal intent. You see how far the winners outperformed the ordinary videos on both.

Scripts

Saved history

Questions

The things people ask first.

Does it actually watch the video?

It reads the spoken audio, the caption, hashtags, duration, sound type and engagement, plus up to 30 comments per video. It does not yet read on-screen text overlays — that's the one gap, and the app says so on every result rather than hiding it.

What makes this different from an AI script generator?

Those start from a prompt. WhyViral starts from proof: videos that already worked, compared against videos that didn't. The comparison is arithmetic, not a language model guessing at patterns.

How many videos do I need?

Three minimum, five is better. They should be about the same specific product — "Sol de Janeiro Cheirosa 62" works, "body mist" doesn't. The tighter the set, the sharper the pattern.

What if the videos don't have much in common?

You'll be told that instead of being handed invented findings. A weak result is a real answer: those videos probably worked for different reasons.

Can I cancel?

Two clicks from your account, any time, no email required. Cancel during the trial and you're never charged.

Does it work outside TikTok?

Not yet. TikTok only, deliberately — the trait model is tuned to how that feed behaves. Reels and Shorts are on the roadmap.
