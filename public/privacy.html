Privacy Policy

Last updated September 2026.

1. What we collect

Your email address, a device identifier generated in your browser, the TikTok URLs you submit, the analyses produced from them, and usage records. Payment details are collected and stored by Stripe — we never see or store your card number.

2. Why we collect it

To sign you in, to save your breakdowns, to enforce one trial per person, to bill you, and to understand which parts of the product are used. We do not sell personal data and we do not run advertising.

3. Who processes it

Supabase (accounts and database), Vercel (hosting), Stripe (payments), Resend (transactional email), and our TikTok data provider, which receives the video URLs you submit. Each is bound by its own terms and processes data on our behalf.

4. Public content

The TikTok videos you analyse are public content published by their creators. We retrieve publicly available metadata, transcripts and comments in order to produce your analysis, and we do not store the video files.

5. Retention

We keep your account and saved analyses until you ask us to delete them. Email support@whyviral.io and we will delete your account and its data within 30 days, except records we must keep for tax and accounting.

6. Your rights

You can request a copy of your data, ask us to correct it, or ask us to delete it. Depending on where you live you may have additional rights under the GDPR or CCPA. Contact us and we will honour them.

7. Cookies

We use local browser storage to keep you signed in and to hold a device identifier. We do not use advertising or third-party tracking cookies.
